SPOILERS — DESIGN, IMPLEMENTATION AND REGRESSION WALKTHROUGH
Do not read before playing if you want the surprises preserved.

CREATIVE THROUGHLINE

The assessment does not measure an empty room. It maintains its attendance.
The player's seemingly harmless answers become administrative authority over
who may occupy the space. Mara Bell's missing record makes that authority
specific and human. Documents, machine copies and physical changes refer to
the same problem instead of introducing unrelated hauntings.

The first section grades honestly. It establishes the paper-return ritual and
three mundane observations. The second retains the original alone/shirt
contradictions but removes the HUD's continuous instruction to check behind.
The explicit hint remains available on H. An unrelated continuation of the
player's footsteps occurs when they stop after sufficient walking in this
section. It does not unlock anything and cannot block progression.

The second acknowledgement opens the actual classroom entrance. The player
walks through a short corridor to Records. The office has an internal glazed
partition, archive shelves, a desk lamp, papers, an emergency circuit, and an
outer door. Its retained copy names Mara Bell and reproduces the player's
original clock/window/notice marks. It also lists actual door attempts and
pencil corrections. Those values come only from this game session: no files,
identity information or external data are examined.

The third section asks which workstation can be removed. The submitted choice
maps A to desk03, B to desk06, C to desk08, and D (no vacancy authorized) to an
administratively imposed desk06 removal. Per-response notes explicitly explain
that override so refusal is not silently misrepresented as consent.

The reader accepts this sheet, then holds it and requests a supervised seated
interval. The player returns to their desk and takes the seat. They may look
around freely, stand, and resume; leaving holds rather than resets the interval.
The authored 18 seconds contain an open intercom, three knocks, a chair scrape,
and a nearby footstep, with ambient room sound withdrawn. At completion one
brief blackout conceals the desk removal and the other chairs turning toward
desk014. A restrained mechanical paper burst accompanies the transition. No
monster or chase appears. The reader then returns the sheet normally.

Section IV asks the player to remember the original desk count. The machine
insists on eight although the original arrangement had nine. A returned answer
also differs from the original pencil mark. The UI no longer narrates that
contradiction; COMPARE CARBON COPY exposes the unchanged original for the
player to interpret. The clock changes to 9:00 while the player is reading this
section. The later ordinary clock question therefore has a new physical answer.
The retained-copy question stays straightforward.

The final record offers four responses. Releasing candidate014 or Mara Bell
produces compliance variants. The chosen release appears on the physical board
and namecard. At the Records outer door the image fades, then returns at the
teacher's position, looking across the room at the next candidate's namecard.
The scanner begins another physical sheet. The receipt identifies the released
and retained person and repeats the player's actual first clock response.

Neither-record / examination-itself responses take a refusal route. The final
report directs the player to the wall isolation switch beside the classroom
entrance. The reader goes dark, the board clears, and the emergency/daylight
circuit leaves the route legible. The outer door concludes in silence with an
unrecoverable attendance record. There is no score gate for either route.

PACING

Five sections / fifteen questions, contextual documents, a connected office
excursion, a short supervised interval, and a physical concluding action target
a roughly 15–20-minute exploratory first play. This estimate is unmeasured.
No generic wait gates pad the ordinary marking/return loop. Most documents
can be lowered at any time. The seated interval is the one deliberately
uncomfortable pause and has recoverable progress.

STATE AND OWNERSHIP

ExamSession remains the sole authority for answers and examination progress.
It retains immutable originals/results and prevents submission during unmet
requirements. An optional section prerequisite='archive' requires MarkArchiveRead
followed by CollectArchiveRecord before the booklet opens. scanCondition='quiet'
prevents FinishScan until AdvanceQuietInterval accumulates 18 seated seconds.
Standing and pause preserve progress. Invalid/nonpositive/large deltas cannot
skip the interval. All four final choices advance through the same core stages.

Game.Sequence owns presentation flags, audio thresholds, blackout, contextual
interactions and final camera staging. It does not maintain a second set of
answers or scores. Scanner.ProcessingHeld is set before each tick from the core
quiet requirement OR remaining blackout. Feeding finishes before the hold.
The processed callback generates the core result before ejection. Retrieval
requires both core ResultsReady and physical scanner Ready. Paper never expires.

Required world geometry changes occur under an inspection or the intentional
blackout. Progress never depends on a disappearing target, a precise input
frame, a correct answer, or an unpaused wall-clock timer. The optional footstep
continuation has no progression gate.

Paper targets stay stable after collection. The outer door remains interactable
until completion. Refusal requires the switch while inside the classroom, to
avoid activation through the corridor wall. Escape during the final camera
sequence still opens a visible pause menu.

Restart recreates only this root, resets progress/resources, and retains mouse
sensitivity, volume and caption preferences. Other games and shared graphics
assets are unchanged.

AUTHORING

Resources/TestAnxietyExam.json has sections with unique ids, titles, instructions
and questions. Each question has four nonempty options and a zero-based
correctOption. Supported anomalies: none, alone, shirt, changed-answer. Shirt
requires changedPrompt, which commits only on reopening after the original was
shown and the rear of the room was observed.

Optional responseNotes contains exactly four nonempty strings and selects the
annotation using the ORIGINAL submitted option. ExaminerNote is the fallback.
This supports the workstation allocation explanation without a hardcoded grader.

Section prerequisites: none/archive. Scan conditions: none/quiet. Presentation
references stable section ids verification, allocation, retention, release and
question ids allocation-seat, release-choice, clock, windows, notice. Reordering
ordinary questions is safe; changing these story ids requires corresponding
staging and physical-clue changes. Core tests independently exercise reordered,
removed and repeated requirement content.

PRESENTATION LIMITS

The procedural classroom and office have roughly a thousand renderers and
several real-time lights. No profiling or Play Mode pass was run for this
expansion. Actual darkness, sound balance, camera pacing, the transparent
partition, final-view namecard readability, and collision clearances need Editor
verification. World text uses normalized physical em sizes; larger readable
overlays are authoritative. No voice recording, character rig, hand model,
real exterior or persistent checkpoint is included.
