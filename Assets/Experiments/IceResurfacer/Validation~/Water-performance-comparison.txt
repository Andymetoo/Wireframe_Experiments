Water redistribution performance verification

Core-only .NET 9.0.14 reproduction of the native long_session workload: 30,090
steps at 0.02 s, 29,999 measured after warmup. This performance fixture resets
the vehicle to the lane start; it is separate from the physically driven full
route validation. No Unity/render/UI work is measured here.

Before -> after:
  Mean measured step:       1.948062 -> 1.086348 ms (44.2% lower)
  Last 5,000-step window:   2.696677 -> 1.673518 ms (37.9% lower)
  Peak active liquid cells: 62,936, unchanged
  Mean sampled cells/step:  5,683.506, unchanged
  Retained water:           504.609897434 L, unchanged
  Redistribution ledger:   2845.553037695 L, unchanged (includes repeated flow)

PASS: six intermediate and final SHA-256 hashes match bit-for-bit over all
cells' scratch, snow, cut, wash, flood, quality, excess, wet timestamp, gouge,
liquid, frozen water, frozen excess, liquid timestamp, and coverage state.
Active counts and every reported water counter also match exactly.

Only Core/IceWaterSpread.cs changed: resolve each cell's exact liquid depth once
per spread step; reuse the identical exponential for cells resolved at the
previous spread timestamp; apply by reference and avoid redundant integer
row divisions. Exact exponential physics, thresholds, traversal/application
order, 32,768-cell budget, 0.10 s cadence, scoring and conservation are preserved.
Two lazily allocated arrays add approximately 5 MB at the default grid.
There are no new per-step allocations. The probe's diagnostic allocation count includes
its own state-hashing strings and is not a native runtime allocation measure.

The paired raw outputs and probe source are in this directory. Native frame
performance must be measured separately after all presentation changes.
