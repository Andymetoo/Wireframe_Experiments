ADJUSTING THE BOARD BRUSH (operator guide)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open the authored scene. Select the machine root (AuroraR54, IceMachineRig Inspector) and press
   "Add Board Brush Rig (keeps the current brush pose)". This is one-time: it adds the rig to the
   brush arm, reads your current arm position/angle/head height as the starting draft, and keeps the
   motor housing and guard plate out of runtime batching so they can stay still while the bristles spin.
   Nothing visibly moves if your arm is posed stowed (as it is now); an arm saved in its working
   pose is shown stowed, because the saved rest pose is always the one Play starts from. (Undo removes it.)
2. The Inspector now shows the board brush rig. Sections:
   MECHANISM (simulation contact, a draft until applied)
     Pivot lateral X / forward Z  where the arm hinges (machine space: X right, Z forward of the rear axle)
     Arm length                   pivot to brush-disc centre
     Brush contact radius         the disc that sweeps snow
     Working angle                arm angle when deployed (0 = straight out to the left)
     Stowed angle                 arm angle when retracted (positive swings the head rearward)
   COSMETIC (never changes contact)
     Pivot height, head height working/stowed, link attach points and thickness, contact-roller offset,
     head tilt and scale, motor-housing and guard-plate offsets.
3. PREVIEW: drag "Deployment %" or press Stowed / 25% / 50% / 75% / Working. The arm, head, link and
   roller articulate exactly as they will in Play. "Preview geometry" switches between your draft and the
   simulation (what Play would use). "Reset Preview (stowed)" returns to the rest pose.
   The preview is never saved: saving the scene or prefab, entering Play, or deselecting the rig puts the
   brush back to stowed first.
4. Scene view (with the arm selected):
     white dot = pivot; green line/disc = working pose; grey = stowed; yellow arc = travel; yellow disc =
     current preview; red dot = kickplate contact point; blue dotted line = body side; magenta = the
     simulation's geometry when your draft has not been applied.
     Drag the green or grey dot to set that angle (and the shared arm length). Moving the arm with the
     Move tool moves the pivot.
5. SIMULATION AGREEMENT: the Inspector says "Draft matches the simulation" or lists what is not applied.
   It also checks reach (the working brush edge must reach past the body side, or the body hits the boards
   first) and whether the visible bristles match the contact radius.
   Press "Apply Brush Geometry to Simulation..." to copy pivot X/Z, arm length, radius, working and stowed
   angles into the scene calibration. It validates first (rejects e.g. a working angle beyond the stowed
   angle) and is undoable with Edit > Undo. "Revert Draft to Simulation" goes the other way.
   Until you apply, Play poses the brush from the scene calibration so the visible disc is always where
   snow is actually swept (a console warning reminds you).
6. Save the scene. Settings survive restarting Unity. To keep them in the prefab instead of the scene,
   apply the overrides to the prefab as usual.

Notes
- Route/GPS compatibility does not include brush fields, so brush changes never invalidate recorded routes.
- Two existing checks constrain where the brush can go: the layout validation wants the stowed brush
  centre left of x = -0.80 (the Inspector warns), and the fixed Q (conditioner) quicklook must still show
  the brush. The brush already sits at the edge of that frame, so moving the pivot much forward of
  z = 1.16 can push it out of view (a pivot at z 1.25 failed this check in testing).
- The machine rig's "Apply config to physics anchors" also resets the brush draft to the calibration.
- Default calibration (working angle 0, stowed 85) reproduces the previous brush exactly; the launch scene
  (no rig) uses the same formula.
- The simulated disc is now clamped at the kickplate at every deployment (previously only above 50%),
  so the swinging brush no longer passes through the boards and snaps back. Sweeping still needs more
  than 50% deployment, so snow and scoring are unchanged.

CONDITIONER ALIGNMENT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Experiments > Ice Resurfacer > Authored Scene > Conditioner Alignment... (also a button on the machine rig)
Shows every measurement below for the open scene, draws the simulated cut / flood / pickup bands, housing
and cylinder lines in the Scene view at a chosen lift, and offers three explicit, undoable actions:
  Seat Blade Edge on Ice (cosmetic)             lowers the drawn blade so its edge meets the ice at depth 0
  Snap Visible Conditioner to Simulation Pose   discards an Edit Mode move of the conditioner root
  Move Simulated Conditioner to Authored Position...  MECHANICAL: changes DrawbarLength; lists the effect on
                                                cut/water/pickup position and on saved routes first
The runtime blade is now rest-relative (authored pose = depth 0), so seating it is honoured in Play.
