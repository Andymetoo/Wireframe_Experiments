AURORA R-54 / ROUTE EDITOR AND LIVE TRAINING GUIDE
Validated with Unity 6000.4.1f1, Windows, NVIDIA RTX 3050 Laptop / D3D11.

Implemented
The existing ResurfacingRoute and F5 component now accept separately saved,
editable cubic guides derived from version-1 recorder files. Raw recordings
remain unchanged. The Editor provides overhead selection/dragging, tangent and
heading adjustment, exact curve insertion, point removal, undo/redo, transition
markers, vehicle/conditioner paths and separate blade/water footprint layers.
Invalid curves remain editable drafts and are refused for live selection.

F5 displays the current/upcoming route within 28 m. Ground geometry starts 4 m
ahead, fades at its ends and respects solid depth. A cockpit/standing/chase card
shows context, direction, upcoming turn, pace and separate vehicle/conditioner
offsets. The Surface Survey retains its ice data and current-position icon with
a thin limited guide, upcoming-turn marker and actual contact envelopes.
Sequential progress uses route distance, heading, signed travel, traveled-distance
budget and adjacent context; it never globally chooses a later nearby pass.
Deviations can rejoin the same context. F6 permits explicit distant leg rejoin.
All of these operations are advisory and leave controls, physics, ice and scoring
under the original simulator's authority.

Workflow and persistence
Record in Free Simulation / Practice: F6 > Raw Recordings > Start New (F7),
Close and Drive, execute the entire route normally, F7 Stop, Save Named Copy.
After Play Mode: Experiments > Ice Resurfacer > Training Route Editor > Open Raw
.route.json. Any compatible recorder file can be selected without code changes.
Edit, inspect red constraints/overlap, Save Corrected COPY. F6 > Training Guides >
Refresh loads saved copies during play; F5 toggles the selected guide. Use in
Play selects a snapshot directly from the Editor. Original F5 Reference restores
the initial source; the initial source is also restored on a new Play instance.

Editor raw files: Assets/Experiments/IceResurfacer/RouteRecordings~/*.route.json
Corrected files: its Guides/*.guide.json; recovery files: Guides/Drafts/.
Player raw files: Application.persistentDataPath/IceResurfacer/Routes
Player corrected files: its Guides/ subdirectory. The panel shows the exact path.
Copy a corrected guide into that directory and Refresh. Runtime editing remains
an Editor workflow. No Play Mode scene-object persistence is assumed.
The inspectable JSON guide embeds the exact original raw JSON, source ID/SHA-256,
physical calibration and ordered controls/handles/directions/intent/markers.
An independent envelope checksum protects the corrected guide. Copies have unique
names; draft publication is atomic and retains the prior backup.

Remaining limits / calibration
The actual operator pattern still needs to be recorded and manually reviewed.
The spline has continuous heading with bounded sampled curvature; G1 joins can
change curvature and may require creep/stopping to set steering. This pass does
not certify C2 steering continuity or full tire/dynamic tracking at arbitrary
speed. Pace is advisory. Synchronous fit/load can pause briefly for large routes.
Major sequence skips require explicit leg selection; no global nearest-loop
reacquisition occurs. Editor undo history is local to the window session.
20 cm contact diagnostics estimate geometry, using the simulator's blade contact
rectangle and water outlet/fringe. They do not measure fluid flux, exact overlap
percentages or the native 5 cm scored treatment. Older recordings store valve
state rather than tank volume, so recorded-water delivery cannot be proved.
Manual contrast, turning pace, overlap and operator-route calibration remain.
No subjective claim is made that the test fixture represents actual operator work.
