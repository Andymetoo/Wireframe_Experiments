DEPOSITED SNOW

The old mesh gave each cell one center tip, with four flat-shaded triangles.
An isolated 30 mm cell became an approximately 90 mm needle to represent the
cell's volume. Low perimeter heights and disconnected lighting amplified this.

SnowSurfaceGeometry now builds a broad, bevelled cap: four shared perimeter
corners and four inner corners, joined by ten triangles. The top spans about
58% or more of an ordinary cell footprint. Neighbor cells share corner heights and
gradient normals, producing a connected, smoothly shaded heightfield. Gentle
low-frequency inset variation keeps the mounds uneven. Angular shaved-ice
particles, pale snow material and low smoothness remain.

Cap height is solved from its triangle integral to represent the cell's actual
snow volume. Interior cells use an equivalent fast rectangular integral; board
cells retain the general clipped-triangle calculation. No snow stock is moved,
spread, created or deleted by the presentation. Cleared cells own no triangles.
The existing .55 mm mesh threshold and .2 mm anti-z-fighting offset remain.

Measured isolated mean depth 30 mm, roughness .7:
  Before: 89.8109 mm tip. After: 36.2589 mm broad cap (66.48% top area).
  Roughness 0..1: peak 35.33..36.67 mm in the tested cell.
  Board/corner integral error: at most 1.15e-11 m3 in the fixtures.

Actual fixed-radius deposits, rather than an invented cosmetic stock:
  0.1 L: visible width .35 m, peak 1.35 mm, 37 occupied mesh cells.
  1.0 L: visible width .45 m, peak 13.50 mm, 61 occupied mesh cells.
  5.0 L: visible width .45 m, peak 67.51 mm, 61 occupied mesh cells.
More snow increases body and thickness; extent follows the existing deposited
field. At very low quantities some volume stays below the mesh threshold and
is represented by the existing ice shader instead. Extremely board-clipped
cells can be taller to conserve the volume within their smaller visible area.

Sparse 40x40-cell chunks and the eight-addition rebuild budget remain. Removing
snow still takes rebuild priority. Mesh.Clear before smaller uploads preserves
the prior wet-recollection error correction. The new mesh uses eight vertices
per occupied cell instead of twelve, with shared normals and reusable buffers.

BIN SPOUT

IceSnowView meters the positive change in TotalSnowCollectedM3: actual accepted
delivery into the tank, after conveyor limits, jams, crowding and rejection.
Particle count scales with delivered volume; fragment size scales with measured
delivery rate relative to vertical capacity. A frame without delivery emits no
new fragments. Fractional sampling supports low/intermittent flow. Pause/reset
cannot accumulate a deferred burst. Existing stock in a running vertical auger
can correctly finish delivering after the horizontal auger is stopped.

The existing discharge pool is reused: at most 360 live fragments and 64 new
samples per presentation update. Whole excess samples are dropped, not queued.
There are still five pools and 2,520 maximum particles in total, with one shared
granule mesh. No per-clump GameObjects, collision simulation or snow transfers.
The spout stays in machine-local space through travel and turns. Existing side
overflow, rejected upper discharge and dumping remain separate cues.

The actual TankDischarge anchor supplies the origin, offset forward by 55 mm to
clear its lip. Its arc aims left/forward below the existing TankInspection
opening, away from the roof and central guard. This corrects the old stream's
position beside the aperture. Its runtime material is slightly emissive so
white fragments remain legible inside the dark bin. Authored assets unchanged.

Use the existing R standing toggle or hold Space, then look down through the
top inspection opening with RMB. No new camera mode or control binding added.
Read-only intersections against 9,024 authored triangles/conservative built-in
bounds found clear standing-eye rays to seven of eight stream samples, from
0.16 through 0.36 seconds of flight. This is static empty/lowered-bin geometry
evidence, not a rendered capture. A full snow surface can obscure the stream.

OPERATOR TUNING

The existing serialized windrowRoughness value is preserved, now labelled
"Mound roughness (0-1)". Only one new control: "Bin spout visibility (x)",
default 1, range 0..3. It changes cosmetic sample density; zero hides the cue.
No delivery still means no spout at every setting. Restore resets it to 1.
All saved simulation and previous presentation values are retained.
