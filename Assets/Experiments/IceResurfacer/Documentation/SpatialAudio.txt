Finding and scope
The saved pre-pass local source confirmed the reported problem: nine of the ten
machine loops, the shared mechanism voice, and all three service-water sources
used spatialBlend=0. The guide-roller loop and three collision voices were 3D.
Engine and water consequently had no physical distance attenuation.
Validation~/SpatialAudioBefore preserves the original sources and baseline hashes.

Physical emitters now use spatialBlend=1 and share one world-meter distance curve.
Only the existing arena-ventilation loop remains 2D. No UI behavior was changed.
No simulation, cockpit geometry, steering, brush, camera/listener code, scene,
material, shader, project setting, package or unrelated experiment was changed.

Sources on machine parts inherit their actual transforms, including the
independently articulated conditioner. World-space event emitters update from
existing simulation event positions; collisions stay at the point of impact.

Sound design and source budget
CreateLoop, CreateMechanism, CreateImpact and service Noise synthesis methods
are byte-identical to the pre-pass versions. The ten original machine-loop
clips are shared across the split emitters. Existing engine/load/speed/throttle,
blade depth, auger congestion/jam, traction and contact volume/pitch formulas
remain. Water components retain their .12 flood / .09 wash / .06 pump targets.
The .22 hydraulic target is divided among the active actuator groups; snow's
existing aggregate target is divided by actual outlet flow rates. Existing
level smoothing remains. Spatial panning and distance change the received mix.

27 persistent AudioSources: 19 machine/ambience loops, two mechanism voices,
three contact voices, two service loops and one coupling voice. One-shot voices
stop before reuse, bounding overlapping voice count and preventing an older tail
from jumping to a new contact. No AudioSource or AudioClip is created/destroyed
per frame or camera change. The existing configuration supplies 32 real voices
and 512 virtual voices. This pass does not claim a new CPU/frame-time benchmark.

Distance and listener
All physical sources: minDistance=2.5 m, maxDistance=120 m, custom monotonic
piecewise-linear rolloff. Full amplitude through 2.5 m, sampled inverse-distance
amplitude (2.5/d) through 80 m, then .01 at 100 m and zero at/beyond 120 m.
At key distances: 5 m=.5, 10 m=.25, 20 m=.125, 40 m=.0625, 60 m=.04167.
Interpolation between those keys is linear. There is no short few-meter cutoff.
Runtime attenuation is Unity's spatial audio, without a second distance-volume
multiplier. The final zero also avoids a nonzero far-distance plateau.

Doppler=0 preserves the existing state-driven pitch during camera movement.
Mono clips, spread=0 and centered panStereo use the camera listener's actual
orientation. Dry output (reverbZoneMix=0) avoids an unintended constant wet bed;
there are no authored arena acoustic zones in this implementation.

The existing AudioListener on DriverCamera is retained. That SAME camera and
listener reparent/move for seated, standing, chase and on-foot modes. No new
listener, per-mode master gain, or pitch compensation was introduced. Camera
distance therefore changes the level naturally, with the existing camera motion.

Measured native Unity DSP output
Validation records the spatialized stereo master through Unity AudioRenderer,
48 kHz, approximately three seconds per probe. The engine and flood loop are
isolated in turn at fixed source volume=.5 and pitch=1 to measure distance gain
independently of machine-state changes. Reference is that source at true 2 m
3D source-to-listener distance. RMS combines the energy of both stereo channels.
Measurements use the saved scene's real seated and R-standing eye positions.
Machine at (-9, -20), heading zero; cross-width listener (11, 1.65, -20),
opposite-end listener (-9, 1.65, 25). All distances below are actual source-to-ear
distances, not distance to the nearest body panel.

Tiny deviations from unity reflect capture-buffer boundaries of the synthesized
loop. The legacy 2D control measured .9993 (-.006 dB) at 25 m versus 2 m,
reproducing the original fault. At 5 m, a source on the listener's right rendered
only in the right channel; a 180-degree turn swapped it to the left, with about
.066% level difference. All twelve spatial probes remained non-silent and
matched the configured curve within the validation tolerance.

Repeat validation
In PowerShell at the project root, with other heavy validation runs finished:
  & 'Assets/Experiments/IceResurfacer/Validation~/StageUnityValidation.ps1' -UpdateOnly
  & 'Assets/Experiments/IceResurfacer/Validation~/RunIsolatedUnity.ps1' -Action SpatialAudio -LogName Spatial-audio-unity.log
  & 'Assets/Experiments/IceResurfacer/Validation~/RunIsolatedUnity.ps1' -Action RunBatch -LogName Spatial-audio-regression-unity.log
DSP recording is guarded to the disposable Validation~/UnityProject copy and
never routes the probe sequence through the user's live audio device. Results
are written inside that copy; copy its Validation~/SpatialAudio output to the
main experiment's Validation~/SpatialAudio after a passing run.
Stage without -UpdateOnly first if the disposable project does not yet exist.

Remaining listening calibration
This is measured native stereo rendering, not a human listening assessment.
Headphone/speaker comfort, perceived loudness against ventilation, mix balance
with every subsystem running, and the desired mechanical-water detail at the
operator's ear still need operator audition. The WAVs preserve absolute levels
for that comparison. No HRTF plugin, wall/cabin occlusion, acoustic reverberation,
distance filtering or equipment recordings were added; the preserved synthetic
point-source design has those limits. No acoustic SPL calibration is claimed.
