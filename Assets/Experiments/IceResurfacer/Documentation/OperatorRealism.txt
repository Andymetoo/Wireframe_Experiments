Controls and viewpoint
RMB rotates the head; release preserves the exact rendered yaw/pitch relative
to the machine. There is no release target, recenter spring or rotational
filter left running after release. Yaw remains -170..170 degrees; pitch
-55..80. Vehicle rotation carries the seat and head together.
R recenters to yaw 0/pitch 5 over 0.20 seconds using a smoothstep. E already
adjusts the blade and remains assigned to it. A new mouse motion can interrupt
an intentional recenter. Only R and New Session intentionally return forward.
The cursor position is saved at RMB acquisition and restored on release.
Acquisition and the next frame discard lock-generated deltas. Mouse movement
outside RMB does not alter yaw/pitch. Guide, pause and focus loss preserve it.
Space raises eyes 0.48 m and leans forward 0.10 m; release lowers the eyes.
Both directions preserve head rotation. Ctrl (either side) now applies the
service brake, previously on Space. WASD and mouse instruments remain usable
in both postures. Left rear shoulder lean exposes the conditioner edge.

Tank, snow and dumping
The previous tank was an opaque solid with a dark patch and top-mounted mounds.
It is now a hollow shell with floor/walls, an opening front dump door, and roof
panels around a black-framed inspection opening near the operator and a separate
conveyor inlet. There is no solid roof/body beneath the inspection opening.
A reusable 221-vertex interior surface occupies 1.488 x 2.026 m. Its integrated
height represents 2.83 m3 at full physical volume, corresponding to the 3.54 m3
loose-snow equivalent capacity through 1.25:1 compaction. The uneven profile has
zero integrated mean. It vanishes when empty and falls as snow is actually
removed. It is a bulk fill approximation, not simulated snow grains.
Discharge particles follow accepted vertical-conveyor m3/s; stopped, empty or
jammed conveyors have no new discharge. Near-full crowding reduces throughput
and raises load. Rejected vertical throughput sprays near the discharge and
deposits into nearby ice cells; horizontal overflow deposits at the conditioner.
The same finite source volume is removed from buffers. Persistent ice snow
can be collected again. Off-ice losses are recorded separately rather than
silently creating or deleting storage. SpilledSnowM3 is cumulative throughput,
not a second snow reservoir. No emission can be sustained without source snow.
Six seconds of sustained blocked discharge latches a jam, stops both physical
conveyor animations, and changes load/sound. J resets only while stopped, blade
out, both conveyors OFF, tank lowered and capacity available. The normal manual
snow breaker remains available for feed assistance but cannot clear a jam while
running. This is an inspection/reset abstraction, not a maintenance procedure.
Dumping retains the stopped/in-zone/equipment-off interlocks. The existing tilt
and timed 0.35 m3/s transfer now open the door and drive a falling snow stream
and an accumulating pit mesh. Internal stock decreases during visible discharge;
the tank does not empty before the transfer. Lowering restores the physical tank.
Four bounded 360-particle snow pools and two reusable mesh buffers are used;
no per-cell scene objects or per-frame temporary particle objects are created.

Measured snow calibration
The original source did NOT fill after 20 yards in the reproducible straight
fixture. At 42% throttle from rest, 18.288 m then brake/blade-out/conveyor purge:
  Normal 0.35 mm setting: before 0.047101 m3; after 0.013696 m3 (29.1%).
  Maximum 0.80 mm setting: before 0.098188 m3; after 0.021871 m3 (22.3%).
  Maximum tank fill: before 2.774%; after 0.618% of unchanged 3.54 m3 capacity.
The original maximum cut treated the screw setting as a fully engaged rectangular
bite. BladeBiteFraction=0.16 now calibrates actual removed ice for the floating
shoes/rake: normal bite 0.056 mm, maximum 0.128 mm. Both nominal setting and actual
bite are displayed. Actual cell cut depth and resulting expanded shaved volume
change together. Loose surface snow remains a separately measured source, so
total snow is not exactly 16% of the old volume. Expansion remains 3.1; tank
capacity and UI readings are not multiplied to hide accumulation. Finish/overcut
thresholds retain their intended response to the nominal range while recording
physical cut depth. This bite model needs operator calibration, especially with
different ice hardness, blade rake, speed and downforce.
The native mouse wheel/drag maps the complete 0..0.8 setting to 1260 degrees
(3.5 full turns), with hard end stops and continuous reversal. Both HUD and 3D
handwheel use this same travel; the underlying state never wraps each turn.

Working width and board contact
The cut remains 1.956 m (77 inches). Investigation found no 25% undersized blade:
the remaining limitations were water and blade sharing one footprint, grid
sampling at 0.20 m and edge softening. Water now uses a physical 2.100 m towel,
emitter and flood footprint, inside a 2.120 m conditioner and 2.130 m vehicle
envelope. Wash and snow collection remain confined to the actual cut width.
The 0.10 m grid gives a 1.90..2.00 m rasterized cutting span and approximately
2.10 m wet span. Every processed centre stays in its articulated physical
footprint; outside-rink cells receive no water. Wider flooded-only cells do not
become scored cut coverage. Cosmetic drops are suppressed near board edges.
A modest 64 -> 61 degree lens adjustment plus real water width gives about
14% additional apparent wet half-width on each side at equal distance, while
the physical water increase is 7.4%. Expanding physical water by 25% would
exceed the machine envelope; no such hidden width or scoring increase is used.
The default close-board fixture gives an 85 mm / 3.35 inch physical untreated
margin and 100 mm rasterized edge. Curves and imperfect tracking leave more.
Exposed stainless side plates have amber lower-edge witnesses. The left plate
can be inspected with a rear-left/down shoulder view without seeing through
the seat or conveyor. The original right/down trail view remains available.
The separate front-left guide has a 90 mm radius at local (-1.015,0.53,2.38).
Kinematic shallow collisions project motion along the rounded board tangent,
apply resistance and bounded alignment, then validate the whole body and roller.
Direct impacts still stop and penalize; repeated contact remains penalized.
The wheel rotates from actual contact travel. It is not the board brush roller.

Validation and limits
Results.txt: 24 deterministic core scenarios, including material conservation,
overflow/recovery/jam interlocks, progressive dump, measured width and calibration,
glancing contacts, close-board margin and a complete driven scored run.
The route uses only controls at the production spawn, no pose/cell/tank writes,
capacity changes or refills. Its outer path was moved 0.05 m nearer the boards
for the finer grid; flood demand is 23%. It is an overlapping regression route.
Observed run: 95.18% coverage, 77.7% finish, 85.2 score, 672.3 L flood water,
0.72 m3 dumped, 1753 seconds and four board-contact penalties. This route is
neither an efficient operating procedure nor proof of physical authenticity.
Unity-results.txt, Camera-results.txt and Operations-results.txt record actual
Unity compilation/Play Mode/graphics/input checks: 27 camera assertions and 19
operation checks passed, with zero runtime errors. A 240-tick working CPU
sample measured 3.697 ms/tick and zero managed allocation, excluding HUD/GPU.
Operations rendering stress
fixtures explicitly seed an initially full tank; they do not validate fill rate.
Core fill/recovery/conservation tests use actual generated and transferred snow.
HUD-player-results-* record 25 passing native GUI checks at each of 1366x768
and 1920x1080, including full multi-turn adjustment; no measured text clipping.
The development-player build passed. Captures show the real seated/standing positions,
inspection opening, outer plate, overflow, dumping and pit.
Subjective driving feel, glare, mouse ergonomics, board following, snow density,
maximum bite/load consequences, audio and full-session efficiency need human
operator playtesting. The CPU sample excludes GPU/display and IMGUI; it is not
a guaranteed frame budget. No real-time granular, fluid, tire or rollover solver
has been introduced. The optional wash circuit and priming model are preserved.
