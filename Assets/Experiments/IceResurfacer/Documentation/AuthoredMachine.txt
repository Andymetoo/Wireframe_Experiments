AURORA R-54 / AUTHORED MACHINE (PHASE 1, EXPERIMENTAL)

Purpose
The machine can exist as persistent, editable Scene/prefab geometry instead of being
generated at Play. The launch scene (Scenes/IceResurfacer.unity) is unchanged and still
uses the runtime builder. The authored machine lives in a separate experimental scene.

Files
  Scenes/IceResurfacer_AuthoredMachine.unity  copy of the launch scene + one prefab instance
  Machine/AuroraR54.prefab                     the editable machine (527 selectable renderers)
  Machine/AuroraR54.Meshes.asset               the 26 procedural meshes (readable sub-assets)
  Machine/Materials/*.mat                      the 12 machine palette materials
  Presentation/IceMachineRig.cs                binds authored parts into the IceWorld contract
  Presentation/IceKeepUnbatched.cs             opt a part out of runtime batching
  Editor/IceMachineBaker.cs                    machine generation (new locations only; no menus)
  Editor/IceAuthoredRecovery.cs                Open Authored Scene, first-time setup, Advanced Recovery
  Editor/IceMachineRigEditor.cs                ownership report and anchor tools (rig Inspector)
  Editor/IceAuthoredMachineValidation.cs       batch proof (isolated project only)
  Presentation/IceBoardBrushRig.cs             board brush articulation authoring (see BoardBrushAndConditioner.txt)
  Editor/IceConditionerAlignment.cs            conditioner visual-vs-simulation measurements and window
  The arena is documented in AuthoredArena.txt.

Workflow
1. Experiments > Ice Resurfacer > Open Authored Scene. If the scene does not exist yet it offers to
   create it once as a copy of the launch scene; an existing scene is never replaced.
2. First time only (when Machine/AuroraR54.prefab does not exist):
   Experiments > Ice Resurfacer > Advanced Recovery > Set Up Machine (first time only).
   Runs the same IceWorldBuilder machine code once, unbatched, saves meshes/materials/prefab and
   places one instance under the game object. It is disabled once the prefab exists.
3. Edit normally: select parts in the Scene view or Hierarchy, move/rotate/scale, change
   materials, replace children. Prefer Prefab Mode (open AuroraR54.prefab) so edits live in the
   prefab rather than as scene overrides. Save. Edits survive restarting Unity.
4. Press Play. IceWorldBuilder sees the IceMachineRig under the game root, builds only the arena,
   binds the saved parts, and batches static parts on the Play instance only. No second machine
   is generated. Exiting Play restores the instance; saved assets are never modified or destroyed.

Ownership (shown in the IceMachineRig Inspector)
  Physics anchors / SimulatorConfig is authoritative:
    wheel pivots (WheelTrack, Wheelbase), tank dump hinge (0,.78,2.46), tank shell (snow capacity),
    conveyor discharge, board brush pivot, guide roller, blade and towel widths.
    The Inspector reports drift > 1 mm. Change the calibration (Machine Geometry Editor) and use
    "Apply config to physics anchors"; do not drag these, or physics and visuals disagree.
  Operator controls / authored freely:
    operator deck, seat, steering column, dashboard, pedals, hydraulic lever bank, water valve
    manifold and bearings, blade handwheel assembly, snow breaker assembly, mounting boxes.
    Move or rotate whole mounts. Runtime only animates lever/valve rotation, handwheel turns,
    pedal travel and plunger stroke, always relative to the authored rest pose. Picking, hover
    highlight, drag direction (each lever's own bank), valve/handwheel turning, the E/Controls
    view and the spool/breaker audio follow the authored controls.
    In the experimental scene the Machine Geometry Editor disables its cockpit placement fields.
    A non-blocking advisory reports grips beyond 0.85 m from the seated eye, grips closer than
    6 cm to each other, and grips outside the body envelope.
  Cosmetic / authored freely: bodywork, panels, trim, and children under any driven pivot.
  Runtime-driven pivots (edit their children, not the pivot): machine root, conditioner root,
    tank door, guide roller, brush arm/brush/link/roller (author them with the board brush rig),
    cutting blade (rest-relative: its authored pose is depth zero), both augers, and the
    hydraulic barrels/rods. Cylinders always span their clevis pins, so moving a pin moves the
    cylinder ("Re-pose hydraulic cylinders" previews this in Edit Mode).

Rebake policy
Shape-defining calibration (tank roof height/offset, conveyor offset, conditioner and water
widths, tire width, guide roller radius, valve stem height) is baked into
meshes. The rig Inspector warns when it changed since the bake. The machine is never regenerated
automatically. Advanced Recovery > Generate Fresh Machine Copy writes a new baseline under Recovery/
and leaves the authored prefab alone. Replacing the authored machine in place is only possible in
Advanced Recovery > Replace or Restore Authored Assets: it lists the affected prefab, meshes,
materials and scene links, requires typing REBUILD MACHINE, makes a verified backup in Backups~,
keeps GUIDs, and rolls back automatically on any failure (details in AuthoredArena.txt).

Reference proportions pass (Editor/IceMachineProportionPass.cs)
Experiments > Ice Resurfacer > Authored Scene > Apply Reference Proportions Pass (machine)...
A one-time, bounded edit of Machine/AuroraR54.prefab toward the reference resurfacer (operator seated
high at the rear, over the conditioner). No rebake: it moves or rescales existing objects only.
  Operator station (whole deck)        +0.13 m up, 0.15 m back: seat over the front of the conditioner,
                                       platform above its raised linkage; seat-to-steering unchanged; eye 2.03 m
                                       (further back pushes the board brush out of the fixed Q quicklook frame)
  Equipment-side rear and top rails    same shift, so they still frame the station
  Right equipment deck                 +0.13 m up, 0.15 m back, 1.3x longer, under the moved right controls
  Hydraulic lever bank (station)       0.06 m lower, angled 10 deg toward the seat, scaled 0.9 (clears the
                                       steering rim at full lock)
  Water valve manifold + 2 brackets    0.26 m back, 0.02 m lower: side console beside the seat, above the
                                       blade crank's sweep
  Blade handwheel assembly             low at the seat's front-right corner; its screw spindle shortened
                                       to stop at the platform instead of passing into the conditioner
Not touched: wheels, wheelbase, tank, dump hinge, conveyor/discharge, brush, guide roller, conditioner,
cylinder pins, steering column, dashboard, pedals, snow breaker (they move only with the station).
Each part is changed only if it is still at its generated pose (parts you moved are skipped and listed),
so running it twice changes nothing. The prefab, its .meta and the authored scene are backed up to
Backups~/<time> Machine proportions pass first. Undo: Advanced Recovery > Replace or Restore Authored
Assets > Restore backup (type RESTORE BACKUP). The rig Inspector afterwards lists the station and its
controls as rearranged; that is expected.
Validation (isolated copy): RunIsolatedUnity.ps1 -Action ProportionsSurvey | ProportionsApply | ProportionsVerify
Results and side/cockpit screenshots: Validation~/MachineProportions-results.txt, Validation~/MachineProportions/

Known limits
- IceWaterService fill ports and level gauges remain runtime-generated (not visible in Edit Mode).
- Labels use the font's built-in material in Edit Mode (drawn through geometry); Play swaps to
  the runtime depth-tested lettering material.
- The E/Controls preset keeps its fixed 61-degree vertical lens. It aims at the authored grips,
  but a very wide rearrangement can leave the outermost grip just outside the frame.
- Emissive materials are saved with the RealtimeEmissive GI flag; URP's import validation would
  otherwise strip _EMISSION from the fixture material.
