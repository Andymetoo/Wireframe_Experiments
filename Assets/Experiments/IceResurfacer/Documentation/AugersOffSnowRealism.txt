PHYSICAL CHANGES

The existing saved 0.080 m3 retention number remains intact as nominal housing
volume. A new usable-fill fraction of .17 makes 13.6 L available below the side
lips. Both the authored tuning and procedural calibration acquire this default
without rewriting their serialized values. This is an operator calibration,
not a manufacturer specification or a new measured housing dimension.

Conveyors get first opportunity to remove actual stock. Stock reaching the lip
starts a finite slump, relaxing toward 75% fill with a .18 s time constant and
stopping below 85% fill. Any volume beyond usable capacity also escapes. This
produces intermittent banks from retained material; it is not a timed snow
effect. Restarting the augers drains the remaining real trough/throat stock.

Each side gets a bounded uneven share (37-63%). Its real landing point receives
72% through a varying .10-.20 m kernel and 28% as a smaller .065-.090 m fragment
kernel. Every kernel normalizes over real ice cells. Board-constrained landings
and the off-ice environment ledger remain authoritative. No new snow multiplier.

Normal banks within the new 50 mm intake height are swept whole on moving,
fully lowered contact. Previously they were rationed over footprint traversal,
leaving residue even when transport had capacity. Existing saved pickup values
(authored 50 mm, procedural 6 mm) now govern plowing banks taller than the intake.
All recovered snow enters HorizontalSnowM3, then the finite conveyors and tank;
it can still spill if input overwhelms those stages. Stationary/raised passes
cannot receive this whole-layer pickup. No snow-hiding or material deletion.

PRESENTATION

Sparse chunks reconstruct occupied 5 cm cells with four flat-shaded facets each.
Crests are irregular, but each patch's triangle integral equals that cell's
actual loose volume. A crest's increased height occupies less horizontal area.
Corners clip inside straight and rounded boards. A zero-snow cell owns no snow
triangles, including beside an uncleared bank. Dust <=.55 mm uses the existing
ice shader; its actual snow texture channel is still updated from SnowMm.

Chunks use reusable buffers that grow only as needed, with eight addition
rebuilds per refresh. Removals have immediate priority and can exceed that budget
to clear stale geometry. There are no persistent per-clump objects. Existing
2,520-particle total pool limits remain; a shared angular fragment mesh replaces
the sphere. Particle size/density are presentation-only samples of actual escape.
