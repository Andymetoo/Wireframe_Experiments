Requirement-to-implementation record

1. Physical controls: Four real hydraulic pivots share one transverse mounting
   plate, with 0.17 m spacing and a common fore/aft coordinate. From the operator,
   the order is VERTICAL AUGER / CONDITIONER / HORIZONTAL AUGER / DUMP TANK.
   Pivot axes, lever travel, labels, moving mouse targets and animations follow
   the new mounts. Lift/dump spring to neutral; augers latch independently.
   Adjacent flood/wash L handles have 0.26 m bearing spacing and independent
   90-degree analog operation. The 3.5-turn blade wheel remains separate. E frames
   the real control bank from the seated camera. Flood labels replace ICE WATER.

2. Surface Survey: The physical grid remains 520 x 1200 at 5 cm. The overview is
   a separate 130 x 300 area-weighted texture: sixteen real cells per 20 cm pixel.
   Thin initial dust no longer dominates the display. Exact-cell hover and F2
   retain real classifications, cutting, washing, flooding and the scoring mask.
   Water-only seams remain wet/uncut; visible water does not earn cutting credit.

3. Stationary flooding: Actual liquid above the retained-film threshold spreads
   conservatively between neighboring ice cells, at 10 Hz with a 32,768-cell work
   limit. Boards retain it; freezing and pickup operate on that same stock.
   Deeper water changes actual depth-dependent optics. No decorative water source
   is added. Executed 5/15/30-second measurements are below.

4. Conditioner overflow: Existing finite trough and conveyor capacities remain.
   Both side emitters use small moving clumps, gravity and 720-particle bounds
   each; the other three pools remain 360 each. Serious escape is more visible.
   Deposits above 1 mm produce sparse 2 m snow-height chunks with 10 cm sampling,
   441 vertices and at most 800 triangles per chunk. Heights derive from the same
   5 cm SnowMm removed by pickup. The shader, overview, mesh and stock therefore
   update from one material transfer. Deep deposits can require repeated passes.

5. Settling/freezing: The 420-second physical freeze model was working. Its old
   optical response saturated at moderate liquid depths, making older film look
   nearly as wet as fresh film. The revised response follows actual liquid and
   distinguishes frozen polish from liquid gloss. No unrelated timer is added.
   Comparisons use 0, 5, 10, 20 and 30 minutes of actual phase progression.

6. Lighting: The scene-specific ambient target is RGB 214/228/245. The launch
   component saves and restores the previous ambient/fog state. Materials are
   not indiscriminately whitened; wet/dry contrast and existing grooves remain.

7. HUD and addendum console: The full interactive rack is now a right-side
   console beneath the survey. It starts collapsed and uses functional miniature
   V/C/H/D levers, adjacent valves, blade wheel and accessory controls. Keyboard,
   HUD and physical controls write the same machinery state. TAB expands it;
   held inspection views clear the side UI. A minimal bottom strip retains help,
   messages and route status. Ordinary messages fade after eight seconds; serious
   equipment alerts remain compact. MESSAGES opens history and visibility options.
   Permanent upper instruments explicitly report actual conditioner UP, DOWN,
   LOWERING, RAISING or PARTIALLY LOWERED, with blade setting/contact separate.

8. Conditioner articulation: Relative yaw is limited to +/-2 degrees, replacing
   the former +/-0.9 radians (51.6 degrees). This is an Aurora compliance
   calibration, not a manufacturer specification. Actual processing footprints
   and rendering use the same close-mounted conditioner state. Mechanical lift,
   hitch and telescoping cylinders remain connected. Chassis steering is retained.

9. Rear glance: Hold LEFT SHIFT for a rearward cockpit shoulder view. Release
   restores the exact previous orientation and eye offset, including an RMB view.
   Q/E/C remain temporary inspection views; C is the external chase camera.
   Overlap priority is C > E > Q > LEFT SHIFT, with one saved normal viewpoint.
   RIGHT SHIFT now owns precision throttle and machinery modifiers. R standing,
   held Space, X recenter and persistent RMB look remain available.

10. Optional route: F5 draws a generated reference rear-axle path for departure,
    perimeter work, overlapping passes, turns and return. Default geometry has
    4,056 points over 1,568.7 m, 1.600 m pass spacing and 4.600 m minimum arcs.
    Spacing respects the narrower blade, not only the visible water band. The
    guide highlights upcoming segments and has no access to drive/scoring state.
    Deviation has no penalty or invisible constraint. It is a reference pattern,
    not an optimizer or autopilot; sequential progress follows its intended order.

11. Dump-area freedom and snow piles: The old stop came from an explicit forbidden
    pit rectangle and body-edge rejection, followed by velocity zeroing. A smaller
    clearance could not remove that barrier. The receiver is now a visible,
    load-bearing grate flush with the floor. Vehicle and pedestrian movement can
    cross it; real room walls remain physical obstructions. ContainsPit identifies
    snow reception, not a movement exclusion. Snow still falls into the receiver;
    water passing through it enters an accounted drain sink. Pit/floor snow uses
    irregular volume-based height surfaces with corrected upward winding. No
    open-drop or tire-suspension behavior is claimed for this grated design.

12. Tank/conveyor silhouette: The tank is centered instead of offset +0.20 m.
    Its crown is 150 mm lower, at 0.875 m above the 0.780 m hinge. Shell, door,
    window, rim, cylinder anchors, fill and collision use authored coordinates,
    not an arbitrary parent Y scale. The oversized overhead pipe is replaced by
    a compact blue upright and short throat through a real rear-wall opening.
    Default outlet coordinates are (0.450, 1.480, 0.470) m in machine space.
    The fixed inlet separates when the bin rises, preserving actual missed-bin
    discharge. The unchanged 3.54 m3 loose-equivalent capacity maps into a measured
    1.931 m3 tapered visual envelope with an explicit 1.833:1 compaction ratio.
    That mapping is fictional visual calibration, not manufacturer capacity data.

13. Wheels/brush: The reference 1.37 m wheel track and 0.215 m tire section are
    retained. Moving each wheel out 0.137 m would create an unsupported 1.644 m
    track. Visible hubs, steering and tire-alignment references remain consistent.
    The actual operator station moves 6 cm outboard, without raising eye height.
    The brush mount and simulated contact move 20 cm forward together. Final
    seated tire sightlines pass all three steering poses: 16/39 left, 10/39
    centered and 9/39 right upper-tread rays. Tapered front wheel wells clear
    the actual tire instead of widening the track or lifting the camera.

14. Visual authoring: Experiments > Ice Resurfacer > Machine Geometry Editor
    edits shared serialized tank, conveyor, wheel, brush and cockpit parameters
    with Undo. Save the launch scene to persist them. A selectable unbatched
    preview uses the same builder, preserving major assemblies and individual
    meshes. It is disposable and removed before Play/window close. Dragging a
    preview transform is temporary; shared parameters are the persistent design
    source. Runtime batching remains. A separately maintained prefab was avoided
    because it could drift from collision, machinery transport and runtime geometry.

Preservation and limitations
The successful chassis driving model, steering, normal blade response, finite
water, snow accounting, scoring and 5 cm simulation are retained. Source/assets,
documentation and validation belong to this experiment. No branch, commit or
push is requested. Other experiments, shared packages and project settings are
outside this change. The final preservation audit matches all 50 source assets
to the isolated project, with zero missing metadata, duplicate GUIDs, source
mismatches or changed/untracked paths outside this experiment.

This remains kinematic equipment with bounded liquid redistribution and bulk
snow reconstruction, not manufacturer CAD, suspension/rollover physics, a thermal
slab solver or full fluid/granular simulation. Clumps have no individual collision;
thin dust remains in the shader. The new grate intentionally represents supported
floor. Freeform preview transforms do not become persistent design parameters.
Custom geometry edits require swept tire/contact revalidation; they do not
automatically reshape every collision hull. The default 1.37 m track is checked.
Operator review remains valuable for control reach, front-tire/brush sightlines,
snow intensity, drying/pooling contrast, maximum blade bite and collision sound.
