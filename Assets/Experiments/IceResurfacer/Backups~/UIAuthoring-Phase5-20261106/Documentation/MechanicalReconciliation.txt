      2.1 m conditioner, narrow front) and put the roller where you drew it. The machine could then
      approach the boards ~0.2 m closer at the front; changes driving clearances, brush reach, and the
      GPS compatibility fields VehicleHalfWidth/BodyFront (all routes need new copies again).
