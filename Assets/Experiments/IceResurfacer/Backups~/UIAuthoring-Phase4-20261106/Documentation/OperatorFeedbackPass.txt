GPS

Driving onto the next 15.24 m (50 ft) of route can acquire progress locally,
without returning to the original start. The limit is distance ALONG the
route, not straight-line distance across neighboring laps. Acquisition needs
the appropriate travel direction, a compatible heading and <=.60 m lateral
error. Current valid tracking wins at crossings; a required reversal cannot
be skipped. Deliberate distant joins remain available through leg selection.
Teleports do not silently rejoin, and projections outside the allowed window
cannot advance by repeatedly clamping to its endpoint.

A yellow machine-relative sight line projects the same rear-axle reference
plane used by GPS across the visible machine silhouette. It moves with the
machine and lines up with the route independently of eye position. It is
shown with GPS in the driver view and hidden in chase view. The visibility
work is bounded to 65 samples; native forward-driver capture measured 48
visible segments and 49 ray queries.

DUMP RECOVERY AND APPEARANCE

Full-bin dumping previously repeatedly added snow to nine tiny fixed patches.
The current native baseline produced a 10.528 m column. Dump placement now
settles the same volume into a broad mound; the matching current-code native
fixture peaks at .910 m. Deep stock resists travel and feeds through the actual
finite conveyors back into the tank. Contact yields and allows very slow
creep; it is deterministic heightfield resistance, not a rigid Unity collider.
See SnowRecoveryAndContact.txt for material balances and driven recovery tests.

The renderer also had a separate defect: shared low corners surrounded a
flat cap in every occupied cell, making even smoothly varying stock look
ribbed. Shared reconstruction and sloped inner vertices now remove those
repeated caps while retaining per-cell triangle volume and consistent normals.
An identical 1.5 m3 stock fixture has the same field hash before and after;
native captures show the smoother surface without changing its physical snow.
The discrete outer footprint can still be visible at very close range.
