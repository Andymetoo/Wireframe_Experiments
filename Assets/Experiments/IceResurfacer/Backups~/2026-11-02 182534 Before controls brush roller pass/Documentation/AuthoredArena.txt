NORTHLINE ARENA / AUTHORED ARENA (PHASE 2, EXPERIMENTAL)

Purpose
The rink and arena exist as persistent, editable geometry in the authored scene, next to the
authored Aurora machine. Open Scenes/IceResurfacer_AuthoredMachine.unity and the whole arena and
machine are visible and selectable without pressing Play. The launch scene
(Scenes/IceResurfacer.unity) is unchanged and still generates everything at runtime.

Files

  Arena/Sections/<section>.prefab             one prefab per section, each regenerable on its own
  Arena/Sections/<section>.Meshes.asset       that section's generated meshes (rink bands, ice sheet, hose coil)
  Arena/Materials/*.mat                       the arena's 20 materials (shared by the sections)

Workflow
1. Experiments > Ice Resurfacer > Open Authored Scene.
2. First time only: Experiments > Ice Resurfacer > Authored Scene > Set Up Persistent Arena.
   It generates Arena/ once from the same IceWorldBuilder arena code (plus IceWaterService's own
   station code), backs up the scene file to Backups~ first, and adds one NorthlineArena instance.
   It refuses to run again once Arena/ exists. It never touches Machine/, recordings or guides.
   It also sets this scene's ambient light and fog to what Play uses, so Edit Mode looks like Play.
3. Edit normally. Prefer opening a section prefab (rig Inspector > Open prefab, or double-click in
   Arena/Sections) so edits live in that section; scene overrides also work. Save. Edits persist.
4. Press Play. IceWorldBuilder finds the IceArenaRig, binds the saved ice sheet, pit snow surface and
   pool Lights instead of generating a second rink, and batches static parts on the Play instance only.
   IceWaterService binds the saved station and generates only the carried nozzle. Exiting Play leaves
   every saved file unchanged.

What you can move safely (cosmetic)
  Stands, seats, roof canopy, outer walls, trusses, ducts, fixture housings and diffusers, the Lights
  (position, colour, intensity, range), banners, pennants, board advertisements, signs, scoreboard,
  goals, cabinet, hose reel, parking marks, glass supports, materials and colours of anything.
  You may delete, duplicate or replace decorative objects and add your own meshes.

What is physical (SimulatorConfig and ServiceLayout decide where it really is)
  Ice sheet; dasher, kick strip, top rail and glass bands; the gate frame (service-bay root, jambs,
  uprights, return walls); service-bay floor, side and rear walls; snow pit receiver and pit snow
  surface; the water station frame (the hose holder, supply valve and drain are simulated at fixed
  points), its supply valve handle and holder highlight. These carry an IceArenaPart role.
  Moving or resizing them does NOT move collision. The NorthlineArena Inspector lists every check
  (rink width/length, ice footprint, rounded corners, each board band, gate opening, floor, walls,
  pit, station) and warns when anything is more than 2 mm off; Play logs the same warning.
  "Restore generated poses" puts moved physical parts back. To really change the rink, change the
  calibration, then regenerate the affected section (Advanced Recovery); the drift report shows
  what disagrees until then.
  The ice sheet's material is replaced by the live spatial ice at Play; to change how the ice looks,
  edit the SpatialIce template material, not the ice sheet's material.

Regeneration and recovery (Experiments > Ice Resurfacer > Advanced Recovery)
  Generate Fresh Arena/Machine Copy From Procedural Source
      Writes a complete new baseline under Recovery/<name> <time>. Your authored assets and the scene
      are not changed. Copy parts from it, or delete the folder.
  Replace or Restore Authored Assets...
      Replace the machine, or one arena section, with a fresh procedural version; or restore a backup.
      Shows exactly which files change, then requires typing REBUILD MACHINE, REBUILD ARENA SECTION or
      RESTORE BACKUP. The fresh version is generated and checked in a staging folder first; then every
      affected file, its .meta and the scene are copied to Backups~/<time> <phrase> and verified byte
      for byte; then files are replaced in place (GUIDs kept, so the scene and arena prefab keep their
      links). Any failure restores every file from that backup automatically.
      Materials are kept unless you tick the reset option. Scene/prefab overrides on parts of a
      replaced section or machine no longer apply afterwards (they pointed at the old objects).
  Backups~ is ignored by Unity's importer. Each backup has RESTORE.txt (by-hand instructions) and a
  manifest with SHA-256 hashes.
  Nothing regenerates automatically: not in Play Mode, on domain reload, on import, when opening the
  scene, or from Inspector edits.

Inventory: what stays generated at runtime (and why)
  Ice condition (scratches, snow, finish, wetness, depth) .... IceSurfaceView textures on the saved sheet
  Spatial ice material ....................................... runtime copy of the SpatialIce template
  Deposited snow on the ice and in the pit ................... IceDepositedSnowView / IceSnowView meshes
  Floor water runoff and floor snow .......................... IceWaterService dynamic meshes
  Tank snow, conditioner trough snow, snow particles ......... IceSnowView
  Drawbar, water and snow particles, driver camera ........... IceResurfacerGame
  Carried hose nozzle, live hose, overflow water and spray ... IceWaterService
  Fill ports and tank level gauges on the machine ............ authored in Machine/AuroraR54.prefab once
      "Add Persistent Water Fill Ports to Machine..." has run (RinkAndFillPorts.txt); generated by
      IceWaterService only on a machine without them (the launch scene)
  GPS route guide, collision overlay (F3), dashboard/HUD ..... route guide, overlay and HUD scripts
  Depth-tested lettering material ............................ its atlas texture changes at runtime
  Combined batch meshes ...................................... created on the Play instance only
