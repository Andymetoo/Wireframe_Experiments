COLD SNAP / AIRBORNE SHOT EXPANSION
Executed 2026-10-10, Unity 6000.4.1f1 URP, Windows x64.

IMPLEMENTATION
- Raised both physical and rendered goal frames/netting to 2.2m.
- Quick LMB click releases an immediate snap; holding longer than .18 seconds
  charges a slapshot. RMB passes on release. Holding RMB before pressing LMB
  selects a wrist shot; releasing LMB shoots and suppresses the pending pass.
- Wrist shots are slower and more accurate than slapshots. F remains a snap-shot
  shortcut, Q an immediate pass shortcut.
- Mouse points at a target on the goal plane, ice or rounded rink boundaries.
  WASD offsets that target into nine regions: WA upper-left, none center, SD lower-right.
  The outlined reticle displays the resolved target rather than the launch angle.
- Holding Space exponentially slows skating and suppresses simultaneous thrust.
- Puck height and vertical velocity integrate gravity, ice bounce and energy loss.
  Shots compensate for travel time to the target height, with deterministic spread
  increasing over distance. Players still physically obstruct shots.
- Posts, crossbar, goal roof/netting, boards/glass, bodies, pads and delayed moving
  goalie gloves react at the actual puck height. High pucks cannot be collected
  from the ice; going over the glass causes an announced faceoff.
- Holding LMB before an incoming puck or skating onto a loose puck can one-time
  it at actual stick reach. One shot per hold; later release cannot fire again.
  AI prepares pass one-timers and selects aimed snap/wrist/slap profiles.
- Airborne puck silhouette, short geometric trail and ground shadow expose height.
  Updated first-person view, reticle, charge display and in-game instructions.

LIMITS
The gesture classifier fires snaps on click release, since the initial button-down
cannot distinguish a future hold from a click. WASD has both skating and shot-region
roles; holding Space suppresses propulsion while choosing corners. The puck has
physical linear motion in 3D, but spin/wobble is cosmetic. No aerodynamic spin curve,
manual high-stick play, new hockey rules, creative assets, audio or packages were added.
Human playtesting is still needed for aiming difficulty, shot balance and control feel.
Automated matches and captures do not establish those subjective qualities.
