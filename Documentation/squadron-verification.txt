IMPLEMENTED
The original flight force model, Training Duel and Courier Cover remain.
Two mortal wingmen fly formation, rejoin and obey Form Up / Engage / Cover Me /
Regroup orders. Tactical pilots use delayed decisions, persistent target choices,
bounded visual awareness, terrain and collision avoidance, speed recovery,
defensive breaks, pursuit extensions, damage withdrawal and bounded gun tracking.
They issue ordinary flight inputs and never reposition an aircraft to follow a slot.

Interception requires stopping two raid leaders before a physical boundary.
Bomber Escort requires two of three bombers to complete an actual formation route.
Ground Attack requires destroying four moving trucks under fighter and physical
flak fire. Ground pilots line up, strafe, extend and pull out between passes.
HUD, wingman status, briefings, objective tracking and debriefs support these rules.
Bombers, trucks, flak guns, road, wrecks and effects use code-generated geometry.

Keyboard/mouse controls are retained. Gamepad flight, look, menus and order chords
use a scene-owned device adapter. Pause offers analog settings and optional Input
System axis paths. Invalid vector-as-axis bindings disable once; unplugging does
not leave held controls. RMB freelook is independent of mouse-stick steering.
See meridian-input-guide.txt for mappings and hardware limitations.

REMAINING HUMAN VALIDATION
Automated and rendered evidence proves functioning simulation and outcomes, not
enjoyment. Human playtesting is still needed for squadron usefulness, mission
difficulty, gunnery feel, HUD readability at other resolutions and controller
comfort. No physical gamepad, joystick or HOTAS was tested. Custom binding paths
provide an extension point, not universal device support. Settings last within
the Air Combat scene; there is no campaign, landing sequence, voice/audio or save.
Seeds reproduce identical fixed-step inputs within a runtime; cross-platform
bit-for-bit determinism is not promised.
