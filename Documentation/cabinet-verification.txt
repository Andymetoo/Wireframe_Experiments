Limits:
- Automated pilots and synthetic input prove working controls and combat, not subjective human handling or fun.
- Human testing is still needed for keyboard/mouse sensitivity, targeting difficulty, escort balance and replay longevity.
- Symmetric imperfect AI pilots can circle for prolonged periods; the stronger validation pilot demonstrates a viable firing solution and win, not guaranteed human pacing.
- Missions start airborne; no takeoff, landing, detailed engine/airframe subsystems, sound, campaign or mid-sortie save.
- Rendering uses the existing machine's Unity editor/device; no broad GPU or resolution compatibility survey.
- Seed reproducibility is for identical fixed-step inputs on the same runtime, not promised bitwise equality across platforms.
